## Minecraft notes (about 1.5 blocks per metre)
1. Two-storey booking hall with reinforced-concrete frame, glazed on three sides, with a continuous deep fascia running across to the overbridge roof.
2. Covered overbridge with a wide roof, stairs and lift towers linking the booking hall to the island platform and the single-sided platform.
3. Long steel-framed canopies with deep fascias and hardwood-boarded undersides over all platforms, with single-storey platform buildings beneath.
4. Blue engineering-brick panels at low level on the building, with white vertical glazed tiling on the concrete columns of the hall and overbridge.
5. The 2022 block: a tall, multi-storey car park with red and dark patterned cladding beside the station, which is what most people will recognise today.
