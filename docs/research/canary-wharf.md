## Minecraft notes
1. A huge single box about 265 m long and 24 m deep, so build it as a sliced cut-and-cover pit, not a tunnel.
2. Two glass canopies at the two ends letting daylight into the concourse (glass roof blocks).
3. 20 escalators and 3 lifts for only 2 platforms; escalators in banks.
4. Platform screen doors along both platform edges.
5. Tall vaulted or flat-ceiling concourse, with an open centre and a park above.

Minimum generic modern big box: a rectangular pit with platforms along the long sides, escalator banks in the concourse, screen doors, and a glass street canopy at each end.
