2. A second, shallower U8 platform crossing the hall at right angles, directly under the street.
3. Escalators between the two levels, and street access via a shaft.
4. Grenander-style tiled walls (unknown finish, placeholder only).
5. A Karstadt-style department store entrance at street level.
