## Minecraft notes

1. Give the station a tall, curved roof: a long, low-arched shell over the platforms, not a flat box. Mark it as "curvilinear" in the build notes, since the exact profile is unknown.
2. Put the main hall as a large open concourse above the tracks, with a wide escalator bank from the concourse down to the island platforms (about 1.5 blocks per metre, so a 40 m concourse is about 60 blocks).
3. Use a multi-line sign band at the top: KA01, KS01, KE1, KT1, KJ15 line codes in their colours (red, maroon, purple, cyan), with "KL Sentral" in bold and a "STESEN SENTRAL" logo.
4. Put the KLIA Ekspres platforms (side platforms) in a separate, faregated wing with check-in counters at the end.
5. Keep the bus hub outside the station to the side with a taxi/bus loop, and an underground walkway toward Muzium Negara.
