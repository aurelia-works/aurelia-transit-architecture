# Teddington railway station (1863 LSWR Italianate, Grade II)

Typical of the LSWR two-platform suburban stations of the 1860s: two side platforms, a brick building on the up/town side, and a footbridge. Facts only. "unknown" = not found in the sources checked on 2026-10-20.

## Minecraft notes (about 1.5 blocks per metre)
1. Two side platforms with a footbridge at the north-west end, reached from the station building side and the road, with a second footbridge further south-east.
2. Yellow-brick two-storey building with single-storey wings, hipped slate roof, bracketed eaves and six chimney stacks.
3. Stucco quoins, string course, and round-arched entrance under a wooden canopy with a timber valance on the central projection.
4. Full-length ridge-and-furrow platform canopy with steel girder supports and Warren-truss pattern, no valance.
5. Arched ticket windows and sash windows in square-headed moulded surrounds; the wing windows are narrow recessed arches.
