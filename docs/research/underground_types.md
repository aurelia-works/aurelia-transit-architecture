# Underground station types: comparison

Dimensions are from the station pages in this folder (Angel, Canary Wharf, Baker Street, Westminster, Rosenthaler Platz, Hermannplatz, Vijzelgracht, Copenhagen). "unknown" = not in sources.

| Type | Depth below street | Platform arrangement | Platform length x width | Ceiling / cross-section | Access | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deep tube | 25-40 m (Angel 27 m; Westminster box 39 m) | Side platforms in tunnels or stacked deep tunnels; cross passages | Angel SB 9 m wide (tunnel 9 m); other lengths unknown | Circular tunnel, 9 m (Angel) or about 5.5 m (Copenhagen tunnels); unknown ceiling | Escalators, often one long shaft, plus lifts | Angel, Westminster (Jubilee) |
| Cut-and-cover box | 9-26 m (Canary Wharf 24 m; Vijzelgracht 26 m) | Side or island platforms in a large pit | Canary 265 m long; Vijzelgracht 125 x 9 m | Flat or vaulted box, width unknown (Canary "cathedral") | Escalator banks, lifts, glass canopies | Canary Wharf, Vijzelgracht |
| Shallow U-Bahn under street | 5-10 m (Rosenthaler 7 m; Hermannplatz U7 9 m, U8 at street) | Central island platform; or crossing platforms in a tower | Rosenthaler 130 x 8 m; Hermannplatz U7 135 x 16 m | Unknown; large hall at Hermannplatz | Stairs and lifts; escalators at larger stations | Rosenthaler Platz, Hermannplatz |
| Modern big box | 18-30 m (Copenhagen platforms 18 m; tunnels 20-30 m) | Side platforms with screen doors, or one large box | Canary 265 m; Copenhagen 4-car, length unknown | Tunnels circular (5.5 m); box flat | Escalators, vestibules, lifts | Canary Wharf, Copenhagen M3/M4 |

## Use in the generator
- Deep tube: long escalator shaft (about 27 m rise, about 40 blocks at 1.5/m), two tunnels.
- Cut-and-cover box: pit with long sides, glass canopies at ends.
- Shallow U-Bahn: 5-10 m, stairs or one lift, central island platform, tiled walls.
- Modern box: escalator banks, screen doors, vestibule.
