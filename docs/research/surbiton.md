## Minecraft notes (about 1.5 blocks per metre)
1. Four platform faces on two island platforms, with a non-stop track between P2 and P3. Build the long island platforms, not side platforms.
2. Cantilevered concrete canopy with a moulded edge over the centre and wings, and no columns in view. Use a flat slab with a stepped edge, not a valance.
3. Tall central booking hall: flat-roofed, white/light-grey concrete with long, narrow, closely spaced mullioned windows and bronze-coloured doors at the base.
4. Clock tower at the left (or right) end of the hall with a set-back upper section and horizontal string courses.
5. Curved kiosk bays and long low wings on each side of the hall, with rounded ends on the platform buildings; the fascia says SURBITON in sans-serif capitals.
