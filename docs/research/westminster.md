## Minecraft notes
1. Deep Jubilee platforms in two separate tunnels stacked, reached by a long escalator run from a station box.
2. Sub-surface platforms in green/blue/black/white tiles.
3. 17 escalators and 5 lifts: escalator banks in the box.
4. Ticket hall at street under a building (Portcullis House).
5. Deep void (about 39 m, about 58 blocks at 1.5/m) as the centre.

Minimum generic deep station of this type: two deep platform tunnels with escalators from a single shaft, plus a sub-surface platform level.
