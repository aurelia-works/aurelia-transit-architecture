## Minecraft notes
1. Long central island platform 130 m x 8 m, about 195 x 12 blocks at 1.5 blocks/m.
2. Orange tile walls (glazed look) over the whole platform.
3. Shallow 7 m depth, so stairs or one lift from the street, not escalators.
4. Mezzanine level above the platform for the old border-crossing.
5. Tunnel-style box with a flat or vaulted ceiling (unknown, mark as placeholder).

Minimum generic U-Bahn under street: a central island platform, stairs and a lift, orange or cream tiled walls, and a U-Bahn sign at the entrance.
