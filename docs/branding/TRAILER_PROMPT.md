# ATA 1.6.0 trailer

About 60 seconds, for Modrinth, YouTube and Discord. Record it in game: a mod trailer that shows the real blocks earns trust, and generated video invents blocks the mod does not have. Use the prompt at the end only for an intro card or a mood clip.

Rules for every shot: no Minecraft or Mojang logo, no operator names or logos (no SBB, NS, DB, MTA and so on), no copyrighted music. City looks may be named by city ("Utrecht", "Hamburg"), since those are just places. Shaders are fine but say so in the description. Record at 1920x1080, 60 fps, FOV 70, GUI hidden (F1) except where the shot is about a screen.
