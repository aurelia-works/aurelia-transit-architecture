# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Smart Station Generator (first version):** `/ata_station plan <description>` plans a straight ground-level station from a short English description (1-4 tracks, side or island platforms, length, width, Modern, Dutch, German S-Bahn or Metro style, canopy or hall roof, materials, ramps, name signs), checks it against railway clearances and the ground, and shows a particle outline. `/ata_station confirm` builds it in batches with cancel and undo. Terrain is only changed after `/ata_station confirm replace_terrain`. Fully offline and deterministic. MTR rail nodes are placed at the platform ends for you to connect. Existing MTR rail nodes are never replaced or removed: one already where a node goes is kept. Operators only. See docs/STATION_GENERATOR.md.
- **Station Wrench:** the station generator without commands. Right-click a block to plan the wrench's description there, sneak + right-click a block to build, left-click to cancel, right-click the air to open its screen with Plan, Preview, Build, Build with terrain replacement, Cancel and Undo. Operators only. It never breaks blocks.
- **Station checks:** every plan is now measured block by block against what was asked for: boarding faces and their lengths, rail nodes at the platform ends, roof coverage, headroom, whether a passenger can walk to every boarding place (step-free, by a one-block step, or only across a track), name signs, no floating parts, and the overall size. The summary shows one line plus any warnings; a failed check stops the build. Island platforms get a warning, because without a footbridge or underpass they are only reached across a track.
- **Station buildings and footbridge:** stations can plan a building (8 styles: Utrecht-modern, Amsterdam, St Pancras Gothic brick, Antwerp, Frankfurt sandstone, Hamburg clinker, Munich concrete, Grand Central/Union Beaux-Arts) with ticket hall, gates, office, machines, kiosk, clock and boards, plus a footbridge with deck, piers and stepped platform stairs. Platforms reached only by the footbridge stairs get a "not step-free" warning (no lifts yet). Checks cover 3D access with a STAIRS grade, headroom over overhead floors and roofs only, shelter from building and footbridge roofs, and interior lighting and furnishing. See docs/STATION_GENERATOR.md.
- **Terminus stations and train sheds:** add "terminus" and the tracks end at buffer stops in front of a head building across the track ends, with a gate line, departure boards, clock, ticket office, machines, kiosk and benches. Every platform is reached step-free from the concourse, and a terminus may have a hall roof as its train shed. The St Pancras, Antwerp, Frankfurt and Grand Central presets are now termini.
- **Build fix:** two-high blocks (MTR ticket machines, the information kiosk) are planned as both halves and checked against their planned neighbours, so station buildings no longer stop half-built. Undo counts a lower half that went with its upper half as restored. Game tests now build a station building and a terminus in a world.
- **Waiting room and departure screens:** larger station buildings get an upstairs waiting room behind a glass partition, with benches, bins, a departure board and a hanging clock. Every furnished station now gets platform departure screens (two on platforms of 48 or longer, back to back on wide islands so both tracks see one) and a help point, kept clear of the footbridge stairs.
- **Wrench presets and guide book:** eight preset buttons on the Station Wrench screen (Utrecht, Amsterdam, St Pancras, Antwerp, Frankfurt, Hamburg, Munich, Grand Central) fill in a complete station description. Operators get a *Station Generator Guide* written book the first time they join; `/ata_guide` gives another copy.
- **Live-test fixes:** platform departure screens now hang from the canopy (two wide, double-sided, facing along the platform) instead of standing on the floor, and help points are fixed to a column or lamp post. Footbridge stairs are a full two steps wide with solid side balustrades down to the floor, so no rail hangs at head height. The footbridge deck runs under both corridor walls, so the stair exits have a floor and the walls no longer float. Station building floors use the plinth under the walls, and terminus head buildings get ground-floor windows beside the entrance. The Station Wrench has its own item texture.
- **Footbridge stairs and terminus roofs:** where a footbridge stair rises through a platform canopy, the part above the canopy is enclosed in glass walls with its own roof, instead of an open stair under a wide floating roof. Terminus head buildings cover their roof lights with a solid roof layer, so the roof no longer looks checkered from above. The wrench screen is wider so every preset name fits its button.
- **Footbridge walkway lights:** the lights set into a footbridge deck are now the building's recessed light, a full block lit underneath, instead of the thin canopy light that left a pit in the walkway every few blocks. A test holds every planned walk surface to a full top face.
- **Train sheds that hold together:** hall roofs are now framed in a new connecting **station girder** block (four colours), so beams meet their columns and each other instead of floating. Sheds come flat, arched, pointed-arch or as several arches, with glass vaults, ribs and a glass end screen at a terminus, and now combine with footbridges and buildings beside the tracks.
- **Mixed looks:** descriptions can mix cities, e.g. "american station, utrecht and hamburg combined" (Grand Central building, Dutch platforms, Hamburg arched shed). New look words include american, new york, boston, british, leipzig, cologne and brussels; shed words include arched, pointed, vaulted and several arches.
- **Simple wrench screen:** right-click the air with the Station Wrench to pick a city look (and one to mix in), through station or terminus, tracks, platforms, length, roof, building and name with buttons, then Place in front of me, Build it! and Undo. The typed description and presets moved to an Advanced page. Plan summaries in chat are short with Build it / Cancel / Details buttons (`/ata_station details` shows the full summary), and a finished build offers Undo.
- **Lifts:** footbridge stations now get working MTR lifts: a glass shaft beside the footbridge on every platform 6 or more wide, and one in the station building behind the gate line. The generator forms each lift in MTR when the build finishes, with landing doors and call buttons linked, and undo deletes the lifts again. Those platforms now count as step-free.
- **Undo fix:** undo no longer drops MTR ticket machines, information kiosks and lift doors as items. Taking one half away used to break its partner through a neighbour update, which always drops.
- **Dev screenshots:** the dev client (`./gradlew runClient`) takes screenshots on request, with options to hide the HUD, set the field of view and open the wrench screen or a book. See docs/LIVE_TESTING.md.
- **Station Studio:** the station generator in your browser, like a structure generator website but running on your own computer. The Station Wrench screen's *Station Studio* button (or `/ata_studio`) opens it: a description box with room for 1000 characters, station mode, platform layout, track and length sliders, look, mix, roof, building and name, and a 3D preview drawn from the game's own block models and textures (Overview, Reverse side, Street, Passenger and Plan views, cuts through the station, boarding-face numbers, Invert X/Y, click a block to name it). Below it: requested and built side by side, what the planner read from your words, and every station check. *Plan in front of me*, *Build it*, *Cancel plan* and *Undo last build* act in your world. It only listens on 127.0.0.1, and only the page the game served can act in the game. `tools/station-studio.sh` runs the same studio outside the game for designing without Minecraft open.
- **Longer descriptions:** descriptions may now be 1200 characters (was 256); chat commands keep Minecraft's own chat limit.
- **Plan viewer:** `tools/StationPlanExport.java` exports plans as JSON with suspect blocks flagged, and `tools/station-plan-viewer.html` shows them in 3D. See docs/STATION_GENERATOR.md.

## 1.5.0

- **Wording pass:** tooltips are short and plain (50 characters or fewer, no full stops), and block names name their city. No lang keys of existing blocks changed.
- **ATA Glass tab (22 blocks):** clear float, low-iron, grey, bronze and blue tinted, reflective, frosted, fritted and wired glass, each as a block and a pane, plus glass brick, curtain wall glass, structural glass fin and glass floor panel.
- **ATA European Stations tab:**
  - Netherlands and Belgium (28): Utrecht wave roof, tree column and hall glass; Leidsche Rijn timber soffit and cladding; Amsterdam red brick, stone cornice, cast iron shed and clock face; Rotterdam stainless roof and timber ceiling; Antwerp limestone, marble and glass vault.
  - Germany and Italy (27): Frankfurt sandstone and iron hall truss; Munich concrete and terrazzo; Hamburg clinker brick and hall roof; a small S-Bahn station; Roma travertine and ribbon window.
  - Dutch and German station signs and the Dutch platform sign use the existing editable sign: right-click to type text. The Dutch column band is plain.
- **ATA Metro tab (27):** tiles, ceilings, floors, columns and tunnel finishes inspired by the Munich, Frankfurt, Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Lisbon and Washington metros.
- **Car stop boards (3, Wayfinding tab):** UK car stop marker, German stop board (with an "H" board) and Dutch stop board (with a red and white stop-here board), numbers 1-12 for a train of that many cars. Right-click with an empty hand to change the number, sneak-right-click to go back. Freestanding on a thin post (faces you) or mounted on the wall you aim at; a sign pole joins a freestanding board. No block entity, no ticking.
- **No operator logos:** colours and shapes only, no operator wordmarks or symbols.
- **Lightweight:** no new ticking, at most six cuboids per model. Only the three editable signs have block entities, the same non-ticking kind as the existing signs.

## 1.4.0

- **Station suffixes (A5):** `/ata_suffix` adds "Station", "Airport", "Port", "Terminal" or custom text to an MTR station's name, per context (signs, displays, terminals, announcements). MTR's own name is unchanged.
- **Per-exit settings (A3):** the station information board's Exits view sets each exit's text, arrow and visibility independently, and can add manual exits.
- **Lift status panel (A8):** lift name, levels served and a status bar (in service, out of service, maintenance), set by hand in its editor.
- **Noise barriers (A12):** solid, glass and solid-with-glass panels in full and half height; stack them for taller walls.
- **Station equipment props (A10):** fare gate (gate, wide, end; place side by side for a bank), card reader (post, wall), booth window, CCTV camera (wall, pendant, dome).
- **Working fare gates and transit card (tester request):** the fare gate charges MTR's own fares (same balance, zones and records as MTR's ticket machines and barriers): walk in from the green arrow carrying a **transit card**, MTR picks entry or exit, and the paddles open for one passage. Every player gets a card on first join; `/card` shows the balance (and replaces a lost card), `/card load <emeralds>` tops up at MTR's ticket-machine rates. Works inside MTR station areas; collision 1.5 blocks high.
- **Passenger information kiosk is two blocks tall** (tester request), screen at eye height. Kiosks placed before 1.4 need to be placed again.
- **Sign pole joins its sign** (tester request): a pole directly under a pole-mounted sign, or above a hanging one, continues into it (`pole_mounts` tag).
- **Concourse board:** column headings, Departures/Arrivals, station summary.
- **Platform screen doors for straight and curved edges:** fixed panel and always-open doorway (opens MTR train doors).
- **Drop-barrier and boarding-step platform edges:** the bars drop / the step extends while a train stands at the platform (client-side, from MTR's arrival data). `/aurelia_live edge` explains what an edge sees.
- **PSD text panel (A17), stand-back sign (A16), train composition board (A18):** typed by hand. MTR does not expose PSD text, through trains or car layout to addons.
- **Calling-point times (A14):** PIDS/CIS "Calling at" can show minutes to each stop where MTR reports the same trip there (option **Times**, off by default). `/aurelia_live routes` shows the data.

## 1.3.1

Fixes for the 1.3.0 release check. No new blocks.

- **Station messages with a manual station name (B10):** terminals and station information boards look station messages up by the MTR station they resolve, even when a manual name is shown; the manual name is the fallback only when no MTR station resolves. PIDS already did this. (`ServiceMessages.messageStation`, `ResolvedWayfinding.mtrStationName`)
- **E-paper bus board text scales with width:** full size from three blocks, scaled down to 0.375 on a one-block board; smaller text never shows fewer rows. (`EPaperLayout.textFactor`)
- **Hanging rods, at most two per row:** hanging wayfinding signs, exit signs, hanging station name signs, direction signs and hanging PIDS/CIS draw one rod near each free end and none on inner blocks.
- **Station information board editor:** the destination field gets its own row instead of being enabled but hidden behind the street field.
- **Viaduct joints:** crossbeam fills its block height and sits on the column; knee and diagonal braces reach the column faces (all four column styles).
- **Z-fighting:** trackside fence and 12 other models no longer have coplanar faces with different textures (`tools/check_zfighting.py` reports 0).
